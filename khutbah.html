Welcome to Khutbah Live Translator

🕌
Welcome, Brother/Sister!
This app translates Friday sermons (Khutbah) in real-time, helping Muslims worldwide understand the beautiful teachings regardless of language barriers.
💡 Tip: You can use this app in any mosque or while watching online sermons.
🌍
Select Languages
Choose the Source Language (what the Imam speaks) and Translation Language (your preferred language). We support 30+ languages including Arabic dialects and Latin American Spanish!
💡 Tip: If the Imam speaks Arabic, select the specific dialect (Saudi, Egyptian, etc.) for better accuracy.
🎤
Start Listening
Tap the green microphone button to start. The app will listen to the sermon and translate it automatically. You'll see the audio visualizer moving when speech is detected.
💡 Tip: Position your phone near the speaker for best results. Allow microphone permission when prompted.
📱
Android Users
Important for Android: Use Chrome browser for best experience. Enable "Desktop site" if you face issues. Keep the screen on and the app in foreground during use.
💡 Tip: Go to Settings > Adjust microphone sensitivity if speech isn't being detected properly.
✨
Additional Features
Save Transcript: Download the translation as a text file
Record Audio: Save the sermon audio
Font Size: Adjust text size using +/- buttons
Clear: Remove all translations
💡 Tip: Tap the ? button anytime to see this tutorial again.
🤲
You're Ready!
May Allah bless you and benefit you from this tool. This app is completely free and made with love for the Muslim Ummah. Please share it with others who might benefit!

About Khutbah Live Translator

Developer's Story

My name is Khaled Diab, an Arab Muslim developer. This application was born from a moment of profound realization during a Jumu'ah prayer in Dubai.

I was moved when a Muslim brother shared with me that he couldn't understand a single word of what the Sheikh was saying during the khutbah. Despite being in the same congregation, praising the same Allah, the language barrier prevented him from receiving the spiritual guidance being shared.

This encounter inspired me to create this tool - a bridge for Muslims worldwide to understand the beautiful messages delivered in our mosques, regardless of the language they speak.
