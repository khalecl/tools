Welcome to Khutbah Live Translator

🕌
Welcome, Brother/Sister!
This app translates Friday sermons (Khutbah) in real-time, helping Muslims worldwide understand the beautiful teachings regardless of language barriers.
💡 Tip: Use this app in any mosque or while watching online sermons.
🌍
Select Languages
Choose the Source Language (what the Imam speaks) and Translation Language (your language). We support 50+ languages including Arabic dialects and Latin American Spanish!
💡 Tip: Select specific dialect (Saudi, Egyptian, Mexican, etc.) for better accuracy.
🎤
Start Listening
Tap the green microphone button to start. The app will listen and translate automatically. You'll see the audio visualizer moving when speech is detected.
💡 Tip: Position your phone near the speaker. Allow microphone permission when prompted.
📱
Mobile Users
Android: Use Chrome browser. Keep screen on and app in foreground.

iPhone: Use Safari browser.
💡 Tip: Adjust microphone sensitivity in Settings if speech isn't detected.
✨
Additional Features
💾 Save: Download translation as text
⏺️ Record: Save sermon audio
+/−: Adjust text size
🗑️ Clear: Remove translations
💡 Tip: Tap the ? button anytime to see this tutorial again.
🤲
You're Ready!
May Allah bless you. This app is completely free and made with love for the Muslim Ummah. Please share it with others!

About Khutbah Live Translator

Developer's Story

My name is Khaled Diab, an Arab Muslim developer. This application was born during a Jumu'ah prayer in Dubai when a Muslim brother shared he couldn't understand the Sheikh's khutbah due to language barriers.

This inspired me to create a bridge for Muslims worldwide to understand the beautiful messages in our mosques, regardless of language.
