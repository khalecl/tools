Good to know

Pages are fetched through a free public proxy in the chosen country, so reading works best. Sites that need you to log in or keep a session (for example Gosuslugi accounts) cannot keep cookies in this viewer, and some government portals block proxy and data-centre addresses outright. For those, a VPN with a server in that country is the reliable route.
